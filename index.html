Our story · Tokyo

Progress does notrequire waste.

Our founders were engineers at a big electronics brand. Every year they watched good devices thrown away. So they started Ketsu-sui: devices that last for years, not seasons.

We don't want you to buy less. We want you to value more.

Broken, then more beautiful.

Questions & answers

Ask Ketsu-sui.

01Why is your proposal ethically responsible?

It respects the planet's limited resources and gives people an affordable option: good technology without spending more money.

02How does Japanese culture influence your project?

Our philosophy comes from Mottainai, Monozukuri and Kaizen. Our culture doesn't just inspire the project. It is the project.

03What makes you different from competitors?

Most companies profit when you buy new. We profit when your device lasts. We measure success by how long our devices live.

04What challenges would you face internationally?

Convincing people who buy a new phone every year, and competing with big brands. That is why education matters as much as engineering.

05Is it realistic economically?

Longer product life lowers costs, repair creates local jobs, and less waste means lower cleanup costs. Durability is smart economics.

06How would you impact local communities?

New technical jobs in Colombia, training for young technicians, and less e-waste in local landfills.

07What ethical risks could your proposal create?

Refurbished devices must be safe and old devices hold personal data. Every unit is checked and all data is deleted before reuse.
